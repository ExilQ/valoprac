# Manual browser and hardware acceptance

These checks are provided for execution. They have NOT been performed in this environment. Record browser/OS/GPU/CPU, resolution, display Hz, mouse polling rate/DPI, render scale, antialiasing, test date, console errors and observed results. Test directly in a top-level HTTPS tab: embeds may lack Pointer Lock permission.

1. Open the deployed URL in a desktop browser with WebGL2 hardware acceleration. Confirm the Korean menu, 3D range preview, four selectable modes, settings and empty/real history. Resize to 1920×1080 and 1366×768; confirm no clipped controls.
2. Select a 30-second precision run. Press the start control, then the user-gesture Pointer Lock control. Confirm cursor capture and immediate horizontal/vertical mouse-look. Verify raw input when supported; repeat on a browser/platform where unadjusted movement is unavailable and check graceful fallback. Press Esc to pause; regain capture with a click. Test permission rejection.
3. Hold WASD. Confirm gradual acceleration, inertia after release, smooth reversal and equal normalized forward/diagonal top speeds. Check Shift, Ctrl, jump, air steering and landing. Walk into walls and cover without penetration. Release all keys while changing tabs; return and verify no stuck movement/fire and no elapsed gameplay during the pause.
4. At the same hardware DPI and sensitivity, physically measure a 360-degree turn. Compare the estimate and repeat across supported browsers. Do not claim matching a commercial FPS before measuring that game's turn at the same settings.
5. Shoot each body zone at close range. Confirm torso/limb feedback without elimination and head-only elimination. Repeat while crouched, while moving targets animate, and when heads peek from behind cover. Check that cover occludes bullets and the head mesh matches the hit volume.
6. Hold fire for a full magazine with both rifles. Confirm cadence, recoil climb and horizontal displacement, cosmetic kick, empty-magazine automatic reload, manual R reload and blocked shots during reload. Pull the mouse opposite recoil and verify compensation. Stop firing and confirm smooth recovery without rewriting the base aim.
7. In braking mode, compare matched trials of release-only and opposing input from similar initial speeds. Check speed HUD, readiness prediction, early-shot feedback and positive/negative threshold-relative timing in results. Confirm full deceleration time differs from accuracy readiness time. Avoid interpreting an unmeasured comparison as zero.
8. In spray mode, aim at the rear-wall center; fire two separated bursts of at least three wall impacts, preferably full magazines. Confirm pooled world impacts, ordinal labels on the results plot, centroid/extents/radial statistics, actual input path and optional baseline ideal compensation. With moving position, interpret grouping as a world-space group rather than purely recoil.
9. In tracking mode, run stationary, strafe, random reversal, stop-and-go, crouch and peek variants. Confirm smooth joint motion, meaningful head tracking, exact cover occlusion and reacquisition samples. Looking away must reduce tracking accuracy. The peek variant uses one target at the real right-side cover.
10. Complete all four modes and use early finish once. Check totals against actual shots/kills, results layout, history detail, per-mode trend and saved settings after a reload. Repeat with browser storage blocked: show a Korean persistence notice without crashing. Record that IndexedDB persistence is browser/origin-local.
11. Click the start/resume control with volume enabled. Confirm shot/body/head cues, change volume, mute, release/reacquire capture and resume audio after backgrounding. Confirm no autoplay error prevents gameplay.
12. Capture a 60-second browser performance trace at 1080p, on hardware suitable for the target: mean/median/p95/p99 frame time, long frames, draw calls and GC. At 144 Hz, evaluate 6.94-ms rendering budget; at 240 Hz evaluate mouse responsiveness and 4.17-ms render cadence separately from the 8.33-ms simulation. Current HUD shows rolling render FPS and p95 rAF interval, not GPU time or raw input-to-photon latency.
13. Compare 60/144/240 Hz display modes with the same measured physical mouse motion; observe responsiveness without smoothing. Browser event sampling is not guaranteed identical. Use the automated fixed-input tests for simulation determinism.
14. Artificially throttle CPU or create short frame drops. Confirm no fixed-step loss for ordinary drops; a >0.5-second stall should deliberately pause with a Korean message. Repeat start/menu/result cycles and inspect retained listeners, WebGL contexts and GPU resources for leaks.
15. Check browser console for uncaught exceptions and WebGL errors across all scenarios. Any critical failure blocks full Phase 1 acceptance until fixed and retested.

Passing Node tests and a production bundle do not substitute for these browser/hardware checks.

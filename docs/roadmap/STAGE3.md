## Quality gates and limits

Core correctness and Stage2 software gates passed before this integration; their original measurements and timestamps are retained in STAGE1.md/STAGE2.md. The Stage3 software gate verifies replay reconstruction, size, highlight seeking, ghost derivation, scenario serialization, actual playable task hooks and existing gameplay regressions. Exact final tests/build output is generated afresh by validate-release.mjs; verify-build.mjs requires all retained browser reports and current deterministic benchmarks.

Physical-device qualification remains open. Headless SwiftShader has severe>500ms stalls; the active movement and exercise input probes suspend GPU draws after real scene preparation to isolate BrowserInput/fixed-step/replay behavior. That isolation is explicitly recorded and is not an FPS or input-latency pass. Real prepared3D, actual browser UI and separate viewport/camera probes are retained. A fully rendered software-browser cold warm-up takes13.2105s, exceeding the proposed3s target; warm same-renderer retry takes441.2ms in that captured run. Do not claim the cold-start target, physical integrated-GPU100ms gate, hardware FPS, human listening, sensor accuracy or input-to-photon latency is verified.

Replays require the exact compatible game/simulation/config fingerprint; cross-platform bitwise equivalence is not guaranteed. Viewer audio is intentionally silent. The ghost is a display cache from replay, capped at20s/600points; the source replay remains authoritative. Canonical VALORANT walking ratioF9 and silent landingF10 are unchanged pending direct reference-game measurement. Jump, steering, FOV and bot calibration remain explicitly provisional. Static technical guide pages remain Korean; the optional English catalog covers application screens and retains known legacy fragment-composition coverage limits.

A8 is optional and deferred: local compatible personal records, replay exports and scenario codes support manual comparison, but no accounts, leaderboard service or purported anti-cheat/verified online score was added. Stage4 infrastructure was not entered while physical qualification and user demand remain unestablished.
